<h1 align="center">🌳 Avelune Resort</h1>

<h2>Introduction</h2>
<i>A Sanctuary of Tranquility, Nature, and Timeless Elegance.</i>

<br>

<p align="justify">
<b>Avelune Resort</b> is a digital resort platform developed to provide guests with a convenient and immersive way to explore and experience a peaceful stay surrounded by nature. Located away from the busy urban environment, Avelune offers a tranquil retreat for guests who seek relaxation, comfort, and a closer connection with nature.

The application is designed to help users discover the resort's accommodations, facilities, activities, and services through an intuitive and visually appealing interface. Guests can explore detailed information about the resort and its facilities before planning their stay, making the decision-making and reservation process more convenient.

Built using <b>Laravel</b> as the main web framework and supported by <b>PHP</b>, <b>MySQL</b>, <b>HTML</b>, <b>CSS</b>, and <b>JavaScript</b>, Avelune combines modern web technology with a clean and minimalist UI/UX approach.

By combining nature-inspired aesthetics, accessible information, and practical functionality, Avelune Resort aims to provide a seamless digital experience for guests looking for a peaceful escape from the noise and busyness of the city.
</p>

---

<h2>🚀 Features</h2>

<h3>🏨 Resort Exploration</h3>

- 🌳 <b>Resort Overview:</b> Provides an introduction to Avelune Resort and its natural surroundings.<br>
- 🛏️ <b>Accommodation Information:</b> Allows users to explore available rooms and accommodation details.<br>
- 🏞️ <b>Nature Experience:</b> Presents the peaceful atmosphere and natural environment surrounding the resort.<br>
- 📖 <b>Detailed Information:</b> Provides descriptions of resort services, facilities, and experiences.<br>

<h3>🛋️ Resort Facilities & Amenities</h3>

- ☕ <b>Lune Café:</b> A peaceful café offering specialty coffee, handcrafted pastries, and relaxing views surrounded by nature.<br>
- 🌸 <b>Lunelle Spa:</b> A wellness facility offering relaxing treatments and restorative experiences inspired by natural elements.<br>
- 🏊‍♂️ <b>Serene Pool:</b> A relaxing swimming pool surrounded by natural scenery and comfortable lounging areas.<br>
- 🍸 <b>Solara Lounge:</b> An open-air lounge designed for guests to enjoy relaxing moments and beautiful sunset views.<br>
- 🍽️ <b>Serene Dining:</b> A dining facility offering local and international culinary experiences using fresh seasonal ingredients.<br>
- 🏋️‍♂️ <b>Avelune Fitness:</b> A fitness facility equipped with modern equipment and surrounded by natural views.<br>

<h3>📅 Reservation Features</h3>

- 📝 <b>Reservation:</b> Provides a convenient way for users to make a resort reservation.<br>
- 🛏️ <b>Room Selection:</b> Allows users to view and select available accommodation options.<br>
- 📋 <b>Reservation Information:</b> Displays important information related to the user's booking.<br>
- ⚡ <b>Simple Booking Process:</b> Designed to make the reservation process easier and more efficient.<br>

<h3>💻 Platform & UI Features</h3>

- 🎨 Clean and minimalist interface inspired by nature and luxury hospitality.<br>
- 📱 Responsive design for a comfortable browsing experience across different screen sizes.<br>
- 🧭 Intuitive navigation for easier access to resort information.<br>
- 🖼️ Visual-focused presentation to showcase the resort atmosphere and facilities.<br>
- ✨ Consistent typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy throughout the application.<br>

---

<h2>🎯 Application Purpose</h2>

<p align="justify">
Avelune Resort is developed to provide a digital solution for people who want to escape from the busy and noisy urban environment. The application makes it easier for potential guests to discover a peaceful resort surrounded by nature while also simplifying the process of obtaining information and making a reservation.

The platform aims to connect users with a calm and comfortable vacation experience by presenting resort information in a clear, attractive, and accessible way.
</p>

<h3>💡 Problems Addressed</h3>

- 🌆 <b>Urban Fatigue:</b> People need a peaceful place to rest and escape from the noise and activities of the city.<br>
- 🔎 <b>Limited Resort Information:</b> Potential guests may need to explore different sources to understand rooms, facilities, activities, and the resort atmosphere.<br>
- 📅 <b>Complicated Reservation:</b> The process of finding information and making a reservation can be less practical when handled through separate platforms.<br>
- 💭 <b>Lack of Experience Preview:</b> Guests need a clear overview of the resort environment and available experiences before making a reservation.<br>

<h2>🛠️ Tech Stack</h2>

<h3>🎨 Design</h3>

- <b>Figma</b> — Used for designing the user interface, visual layout, wireframes, and overall UI/UX experience.

<h3>💻 Front-End</h3>

- <b>HTML5</b> — Used to structure the web pages and application content.
- <b>Tailwind CSS</b> — Used to style the interface and build responsive layouts efficiently.
- <b>JavaScript</b> — Used to provide interactive functionality and dynamic behavior on the website.

<h3>⚡ Back-End & Framework</h3>

- <b>PHP</b> — Used as the main server-side programming language.
- <b>Laravel</b> — Used as the main web framework for application structure, routing, controllers, and backend functionality.

<h3>🛢️ Database</h3>

- <b>MySQL</b> — Used to store and manage application data such as users, rooms, facilities, and reservation information.
